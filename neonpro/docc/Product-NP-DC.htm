Standard NP DC Series

-60% energy saving over conventional ferrous core transformers.
- Even and steady brightness along the neon tubes.
- Completely sealed and waterproof, suitable for indoor/outdoor installation.
- Safe and reliable with built-in open circuit protection and thermal protection.
- Support for connection over 100 meters of high voltage output wire.
- More suitable for signs with neon-red tubes or tubes with no mercury.
(Note: Age the neon tubes thoroughly before installation)
