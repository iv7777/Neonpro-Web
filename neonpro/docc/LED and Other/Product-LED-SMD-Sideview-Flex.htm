SMD Sideview Flex
Features:
- Linear separable LED strip on flexible printed circuit board with self-adhesive tape back.
- Separable every 3 LEDs, after cut, the remaining can be used separately or rejoined.
- Light emits from the side, which gives the contour a corona effect.
- Available in various single colors.
Applications:
- Small letters and illuminated sign.
- Mood lighting.
Basic Technical Date:
- Standard length: 5 meters/
- Ribbon width: 5mm
- Working voltage: 12V DC
- Working current: 340-400mA a meter
- Entire ribbon LED quantity:60 LEDs per meter
