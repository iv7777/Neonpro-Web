Features:
- 50% energy saving over conventional transformers.
- No gas tubes discoloration or mercury migration, Ideal for mercury tubes.
- Completely sealed and waterproof, suitable for indoor/outdoor installation.
- Safe and reliable with built-in open circuit protection and thermal protection.
- Support for connection not exceeding 2 meters of high voltage output wire.
Environmental Specifications :
- Operating temperature: -25℃~+40℃
- Cooling: Convection
- Relative humidity: 5% to 100%
- Installation: Indoor or Outdoor
