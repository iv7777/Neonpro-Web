XPS 40mA Series EMC & LVD Tested Models 40mA
Features:
- Output current is 40mA, suitable for Cold Cathode Lighting or neon tube>Dia. 12mm.
- High power factor and low electromagnetic disturbance measuring up to EMC standards.
- Output over voltage protection.
- Fully sealed and waterproof,Ideal for both indoor and outdoor use.
- No mercury migration or " jelly-bean" effects.
- Specially positioned GTO cables, one each side of the casing reduce the possibility of arcing, sparking and high-voltage cross-over etc.
Environmental Specifications :
- Operating temperature: -25℃ ~ +40℃
- Cooling:Convection
- Relative humidity:5% to 100%
- Installation:Indoor or Outdoor
Certificates & Standards :
- CE-EMC:EN 55015; EN 61547; EN 61000-3-2; EN 61000-3-3
- CE-LVD:EN 61347-2-10; EN 61347-1
