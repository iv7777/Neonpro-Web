Me 20mA Series EMC & LVD Tested Models 20mA
Features:
- Electronic type neon power supply.
- Environment friendly, RoHS compliant.
- Measuring up to latest CE standard.
- High power factor and low electromagnetic disturbance.
- Earth-leakage protection.
- Open circuit protection.
- Fully sealed and waterproof.
- No mercury migration or "jelly-bean" effect.
Environmental Specifications :
- Operating temperature: -25℃~+40℃
- Cooling: Convection
- Relative humidity: 5% to 100%
- Installation: Indoor or Outdoor
Certificates & Standards :
- TüV-CE
- TüV-EMC: EN 55015, EN 61547; EN 61000-3-2; EN 61000-3-3
- TüV-LVD: EN 61347-2-10; EN 61347-1
- SGS-RoHS: 2002/95/EC
