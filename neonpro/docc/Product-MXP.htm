MXP Series(Metal Case Electronic NeonPower Supply)

MXP Series ------Metal Case Electronic NeonPower Supply

Features:
- Innovative high reliability circuitry design with electronic magnetic flux leakage transformer inside an aluminum case.
- Symmetrical wave form output, zero mercury migration.
- High constant current output up to 40mApeak,good for neon or cold cathode tubes.
- With substantial aluminum heat sink housing, designing for high ambient temperature area or poor convection applications.
- Fully sealed and waterproof, good for outdoor applications.
Environmental Specifications :
- Operating temperature: -30℃ ~ 50℃@full load
- Cooling: Convection
- Relative humidity: 5% - 100%
