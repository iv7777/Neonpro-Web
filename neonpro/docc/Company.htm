About NeonPro

ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturer of neon products.

NeonPro Co. is an ISO 9001:2015 certified and biggest manufacturer of neon products in China. As the market leader of designing and making Electronic Power Supply and Neon Electrodes for sign applications, we produce over 3,000,000 units of high quality UL, CSA, EK, TUV CE, RoHS approved electronic neon and LED power supply, 20,000,000 pcs neon electrodes annually plus a full spectrum of other sophisticated sign components and accessories. Our products are currently being used in many different countries under different technical standards and various climate conditions.

Since inception, we started with the vision of creating high standard, cost effective, and comprehensive products that sign makers could use to manufacture professional electrical signs. We are currently operating a plant in GuangDong with workshop area over 15000m² and 700+ employees with offices in United States. In addition, we also offer comprehensive OEM & ODM services to our partners.

NeonPro is a trade name (DBA) of Hyrite Lighting Company, which sells its LED lighting products under the Hyrite brand at www.hyrite.com. Our U.S. office, HLN Illumination Co., handles enquiries from North America.
